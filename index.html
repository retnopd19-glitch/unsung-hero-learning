YOUR LEARNING SPACE

Welcome, Learners!

In this unit, you will explore how a small act of kindness can become a meaningful personal story.

Watch: Unsung Hero

Watch the video carefully. Think about the actions, emotions, and message of the story.

Your Activities

Activity 1

Think about kindness and your own experiences.

Activity 2

Explore how a personal narrative is organized.

Activity 3

Notice how writers express feelings through actions.

Activity 4

Write and develop your own personal narrative.

ACTIVITY 1

Think About Kindness

After watching the video, reflect on these questions.

1. What small acts of kindness did you notice in the video?
2. How did the main character's actions affect other people?
3. Have you ever experienced or witnessed a similar act of kindness?

ACTIVITY 2

Explore Personal Narratives

A personal narrative tells a story about an experience. Think about how events are introduced, developed, and concluded.

Consider these questions:

1. Where and when did the experience happen?
2. Who was involved in the experience?
3. What happened first, next, and finally?
4. Why was this experience meaningful to the writer?

ACTIVITY 3

Show, Don't Just Tell

Writers can communicate emotions through actions, gestures, and details instead of simply naming a feeling.

Example:
Instead of: "I was nervous."
Try: "My hands trembled as I looked at the departure screen."

"She was very grateful."

ACTIVITY 4

Your Personal Narrative

Write about a meaningful experience involving kindness. You can describe an experience when someone helped you, or when you helped someone else.

Your writing is saved in this browser on this device. It is not automatically sent to your teacher.

TEACHER RESOURCES

Teacher Area

Unit Overview

Topic: A Small Act of Kindness

Text type: Personal Narrative

Target learners: First-year EFL student teachers (B1–B2)

Duration: Two meetings (2 × 100 minutes)

Learning Objectives

  • Organize events in a meaningful sequence.
  • Express emotions through actions and descriptive details.
  • Communicate the significance of a personal experience.
  • Develop and revise a personal narrative.

Teaching Resources

Video: Thai Life Insurance – Unsung Hero

Approach: Text-Driven Learning (Tomlinson, 2019)

Suggested procedure: Engage learners with the video, invite personal reflection, explore narrative models, guide noticing, and support learners in developing and revising their own stories.

Feedback Reminder

Allow learners to develop and revise their writing before providing teacher feedback. Encourage reflection and learner autonomy during the process.
